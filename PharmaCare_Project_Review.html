1 Abstract

PharmaCare Plus is a comprehensive web-based Pharmacy Management System designed to streamline pharmacy operations, enhance patient care, and improve medication management.


Built using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js), this system provides a unified platform for pharmacists, administrators, and customers to manage prescriptions, inventory, orders, and health records.


Objective: To replace traditional manual pharmacy operations with an automated, secure, and user-friendly digital solution that reduces errors, improves efficiency, and enhances the overall healthcare experience.
